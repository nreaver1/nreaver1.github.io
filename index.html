OSRS Luck Tracker

Shows how lucky or unlucky an Old School RuneScape player was for every collection log drop. It compares their kill count with the drop rate and with every other tracked player.

  • A RuneLite plugin in Java that reads the in-game collection log and reports drops as they happen
  • Six Supabase Edge Functions in TypeScript, with token registration and anti-cheat checks on incoming drops
  • A luck calculation engine made of pure functions and checked against known probability values
  • A Next.js App Router frontend with player search and side-by-side comparison of up to five players

KeepTrack

Nexus

A campaign management system for a Dungeons & Dragons group, with a party roster, a treasury ledger, a loot tracker and session logs. Everyone can view it, and only an admin can make changes.

  • Read-only public mode, with editing locked behind admin login
  • Treasury that handles custom currencies, transactions and loot splits
